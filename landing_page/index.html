Thinking scientifically about your skin.

Everyone in the world has their own special skin.

This makes it hard to solve your acne or skin problems with a single product in your regimen. You may have to try several products, different dosages, or even more complex routines before something starts to work with your skin.

On top of that...

There are so many factors to keep track of that affect skin/acne.
    • You started going to the gym and sweating more.
    • You didn't wash your bed sheets this week.
    • Your new regimen dehyrdates your skin and you'll also need to moisturize.
    • You may have tried this regimen before but forget the effects.
    • And other conditions such as: Skin Congestion, Oily Skin, Dry Skin, Anti-Aging/Wrinkles, Crows Feet, Eczema/Dermatitis, Rosacea, Psoriasis, etc.

Get rid of your acne & skin issues by tracking your skincare routine’s effectiveness.

Based on the Scientific Method, the SkinTheory app is your scientific skincare notepad. Experiment with skin & acne regimens, routinely take photos and written logs, find your scientifically ideal skincare regimen.

The SkinTheory Method involves 3 steps:

1. Find a skin or acne solution you like.

Forming Your Hypothesis.

Maybe you want to see if consuming dairy is causing your acne. So, in the Regimen text box input your experiment:
"No dairy consumption. x2 weeks until "

Note: Adding a time period is important!

2. Do your regimen and log it.

Running Your Experiment
and Recording The Data.

Take pictures of your acne daily and write about how your skin looks and feels in the Skin Status text box.

3. Stick with your regimen or tweak it.

Analyzing your results.

Use the timeline page to see your progess over the last 2 weeks...
Has your acne improved? Stick with it!
Has your acne worsened? Maybe tweak your regmien or try a whole new iteration and restart the cycle!

Your goal is to delete the SkinTheory App 🚮

That sounds weird, but once you've mastered the SkinTheory Method, your skin will be happy and clear, and you won't need our help anymore!
Best of luck with your experiments. 🔬
