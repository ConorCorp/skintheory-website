Thinking scientifically about your acne.

Everyone in the world has their own special skin.

That makes it hard to solve your acne with a single product regimen. You may have to try several of products, different dosages, or even more complex routines before something starts to work with your skin.

This is not made easy.

Many factors can make it tough to select the right acne regimen.
    • The regmien actually worked, but:
      • you started going to the gym and sweating more.
      • you forgot to wash your bed sheets this week.
      • it dried your skin out and you also need to moisturize.
    • The regmien doesn't work, but:
      • you've never not used it.
      • celebrities or influencers are touting these products for $$$.
      • it just has cool marketing.

SkinTheory is a framework based on the Scientific Method for intelligently finding your personal acne regimen.

By trying your own controlled regimen and product trials at home and analyzing the results with the SkinTheory app, you can confidently find your permanent acne solution. This is as easy as regularly taking photos and describing your skin using log entries.
Each log entry will you get closer to understanding and controlling your acne.

The SkinTheory Method involves 3 steps:

1. Find an acne solution you like.

Forming Your Hypothesis.

Maybe you want to see if consuming dairy is causing your acne. So, in the Regimen text box input your experiment:
"No dairy consumption. x2 weeks until "

Note: Adding a time period is important!

2. Do your regimen and log it.

Running Your Experiment
and Recording The Data.

Take pictures of your acne daily and write about how your skin looks and feels in the Skin Status text box.

3. Stick with your regimen or tweak it.

Analyzing your results.

Use the timeline page to see your progess over the last 2 weeks...
Has your acne improved? Stick with it!
Has your acne worsened? Maybe tweak your regmien or try a whole new iteration and restart the cycle!
